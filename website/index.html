Endelea

Your gym workout log: programs, guided sessions and progress.

  • ProgramsBuild your programs and workouts, with exercises, sets and rest times.
  • Guided sessionsCheck off each set; the rest timer starts on its own and tells you when it is over.
  • ProgressHistory, personal records and progress charts for every exercise.
  • Offline and privateYour data stays on your phone. No account, no server, no ads.
  • Optional syncIf you want, a backup copy in a hidden folder of your own Google Drive.

Download for Android

The app is free. Its source code is on GitHub. Read the privacy policy.
